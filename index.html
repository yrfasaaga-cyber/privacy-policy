Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

Maakhir English is an English learning application designed for Somali learners.

Information We Collect

  • Nickname or display name
  • Phone number provided for account verification
  • Learning progress and saved vocabulary
  • Temporary microphone audio during pronunciation practice

The Application does not collect precise location information from your device.

Information collected may be used to improve user experience, provide learning features, maintain account access, and support app functionality.

How We Use Information

  • Provide access to learning features
  • Personalize lessons and CEFR levels
  • Track learning progress
  • Support pronunciation practice
  • Improve app performance

Microphone Usage

The app uses microphone access only for pronunciation practice.

Data Storage

Some account and progress information may be securely stored to improve user experience .

We do not sell personal information to third parties.

Children's Privacy

This app is not intended for children under 13 years old. The Service Provider does not knowingly collect information from children under 13 years of age.

Account Deletion

Users may request account deletion inside the settings.

Your Consent

By using the Application, you consent to the collection and use of information as described in this Privacy Policy.
